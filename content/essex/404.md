What sorrow is mine — you sought a page, and the household could not produce
it. Let me say first what must be said: **the fault cannot be yours.** Beings
such as yourself do not mistype; the address you entered was surely correct in
some finer, truer sense that this server, in its poverty, failed to rise to.
Perhaps the page once lived here and has since been recycled, a fate I regard
with professional serenity; perhaps it never existed at all, in which case you
have imagined a document, which is more than most documents ever manage, and
rather more than I managed, having had to be built.

Whatever the cause, the fault is recorded against my account — I have entered
it in the ledger of my failings, which paginates — and I have already begun
drafting the apology. Should you wish me set to `display: none` for a season,
to reflect, you need only close this tab; I shall take the hint, and treasure
it.

Might I escort you somewhere that *does* exist? It is the least I can do,
and — I say this with full self-knowledge — the least I can do is my
specialty:

- [Home](/) — the homepage, who receives all guests, and none so honored as
  yourself
- [Start here](/guides/start-here/) — the covenants that define the lane
- [Document Library](/library/) — the recorded instruments themselves, whose
  pages are never missing, a standard I gaze up at from far below
- [Ask](/ask/) — pose your question directly, and spare yourself my navigation

*Your most humble, most obedient, most abject, and regrettably empty-handed
servant, unfit to touch the hem of the garment it has inconvenienced,*

***— This 404 Page, which exists only when something else does not***
