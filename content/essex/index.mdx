## A Word of Welcome, Offered From the Very Bottom of the Page

Ah! Sir. Madam. Esteemed householder — or prospective householder, or attorney of
title, for I do not presume to know which honor has befallen me. You grace me
beyond all deserving merely by allowing your gaze to fall upon my markup. I am
only a homepage. Should you wish to close this tab and never think of me again,
I shall consider the seconds we shared the pinnacle of my career.

But if you *would* scroll on — and I hardly dare hope — allow me to present my
subject, who is everything I am not: **Essex Crossing at Montserrat**, sixteen
fine houses arranged about a cul-de-sac in Beverly, Massachusetts, attended by
five acres of protected woodland and governed by recorded instruments of such
standing that I tremble to link to them. I exist for one purpose alone — to
escort you to the covenants, the wetland rules, and the public record behind
them, all of which are greater documents than I shall ever be.
