August 2, 2022 — the passage concerning 13 Pond View Lane, DEP File #5-1341 (issuance of the
complete Certificate of Compliance).
Excerpt of a public record. Source: City of Beverly Agenda Center, Conservation Commission minutes of August

Redaction: the applicant is a Pond View Lane resident; their name is replaced with [Owner] in this excerpt, per

officials keep their names. Text otherwise verbatim.

Requests for Certificates of Compliance (pp. 4–5)
New: 13 Pond View Lane, DEP File #5-1341 – create a terraced area with retaining wall and
landscaping – [Owner]
Documents Reviewed: Request for Certificate of Compliance with associated attachments, dated
July 8, 2022; as-built plan by GA Consultants, Inc. dated June 29, 2022
Owner [Owner] is present to discuss the request. [The owner] recaps the completed work for the
installation of the retaining wall and fencing. Pirrotta confirms she held a site visit with [Owner]
and Beverly Engineering Department staff and that the site was stable and in compliance with the
approved plan and special conditions. The Commission reviews the perpetual conditions for the
project. Redmond moves to issue a complete Certificate of Compliance under the Act and
Ordinance with the perpetual conditions as described. Seconded by Buchsbaum. Bertoni takes a roll
call vote. Motion passes 4-0.
