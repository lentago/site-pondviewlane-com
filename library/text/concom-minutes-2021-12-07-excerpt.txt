Requests for Determination of Applicability, item (d) — pp. 4–5
d. New: 2 Pond View Lane – after the fact patio construction and landscaping – [Owners]
Documents Reviewed: Request for Determination of Applicability application and attachments, by
[Owners], dated November 19, 2021
Pirrotta reads the legal notice. Owner [Owner] is present and summarizes their completed project,
for which they are now seeking after-the-fact approval. Bertoni asks whether the patio is
permeable; [Owner] describes the ¼-inch gaps, allowing water to infiltrate. Buchsbaum asks about
the plants that were installed. Bertoni asks for a file copy of the plan to include the 50-Foot No
Build Zone. Bertoni suggests conditions for the project. Buchsbaum moves to issue a Negative 3
Determination, with the conditions as discussed. Seconded by Alden - St. Pierre. Bertoni takes a
roll call vote. Motion passes 4-0.
